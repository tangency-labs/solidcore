#### WR-754. `SmFace::SectorClassifyCurveOn` dots binormals against raw `sCurvePV[1]` instead of unitized `sCrvTan`
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Intersections and curve classification
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:13372-13377`, `SmFace::SectorClassifyCurveOn` (`[230227_crease]`) at v1.0.1 (`3fd5b9548d19aa8c12c29bc9637a96d42a06f8a5`).
- **What happens:** The `[230227_crease]` fast path copies `sCurvePV[1]` into `sCrvTan`, calls `sCrvTan.Unitize()` (`:13372-13374`), and then leaves `sCrvTan` unused, computing `dDot1 = sBin1.Dot( sCurvePV[1] )` and `dDot2 = sBin2.Dot( sCurvePV[1] )` (`:13376-13377`) before comparing against `+-0.1`. Because `sCurvePV[1]` is un-unitized, the `0.1` cosine threshold scales inversely with `|C'(t)|`: when `|C'(t)| >> 1`, a sub-tolerance tangent graze (`< 0.1 deg`) exceeds `0.1` on iteration `lCount == 0` and exits before the step-off loop leaves the edge/vertex tolerance zone, making sector classification depend on curve parameterization speed; when `|C'(t)| < 0.1`, a perpendicular curve never takes the intended fast path.
- **Trigger:** Any curve crossing or grazing a face boundary where `|C'(t)|` differs substantially from `1` — e.g. circles/ellipses parameterized in degrees of large radius, lines with non-unit velocity, or B-splines over short knot spans. Reached in `tangency-labs/solidcore#2927` (`flanged_spool.step` boolean cuts across multi-hole flanges) and isolated in `wr754_sector_classify_crease_dots_unitized_curve_tangent` (`crates/solidcore/src/query/face/p3.rs`), where reparameterizing the same quadratic Bezier over `[0, 1e-3]` instead of `[0, 1]` flips `inside` from `false` to `true` without the fix.
- **How found:** `tangency-labs/solidcore#2927`, traced in `query::face::p3::sector_classify_curve_on` and verified directly in `source/SMLib/src/SmFace.cpp:13372-13377`.
- **Confirmed:** yes, directly visible in the pinned C++ source (`sCrvTan.Unitize()` followed immediately by `sBin1.Dot( sCurvePV[1] )` and `sBin2.Dot( sCurvePV[1] )` at `SmFace.cpp:13372-13377`) and reproduced by `wr754_sector_classify_crease_dots_unitized_curve_tangent` in `crates/solidcore/src/query/face/p3.rs`. Fixing the dot products to use `sCrvTan` also restores scale invariance on seven `1e-3`-scale faces in `query.face_p3` where `|sCurvePV[1]| ~ 1e-3 < 0.1` previously missed the fast path that their `1.0`-scale counterparts took (`adv_sec_b2_e3_m1_f0.5_c1_t0.5_0.0`: `[1,1,5] -> [1,0,0]`; `adv_sec_b2_e2_m2_f0.5_c0_t0.25_0.75`, `adv_sec_b2_e3_m2_f0.5_c1_t1.0_0.0`, `gen0068`, `gen0108`, `gen0247`: `[0,0,0] -> [1,0,0]`; `gen0583`: `[1,1,5] -> [1,1,2]`).
