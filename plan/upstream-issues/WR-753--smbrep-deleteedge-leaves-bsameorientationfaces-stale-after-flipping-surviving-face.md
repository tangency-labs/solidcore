#### WR-753. `SmBrep::DeleteEdge` leaves `bSameOrientationFaces` stale after flipping the surviving face
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Topology, attributes, solvers
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:5872, 6194-6197, 6526-6530`, `SmBrep::DeleteEdge` at v1.0.1 (`3fd5b9548d19aa8c12c29bc9637a96d42a06f8a5`).
- **What happens:** `bSameOrientationFaces` is computed before face-surface swapping (`:5872`: `pFaceuse->GetOrientation() == pFURad->GetOrientation()`). When merging an outer loop with an inner loop (`:5889-5893`), the face owning the inner loop is forced to be `pSurvF` regardless of `pOptSurfaceToKeep`. If `pDelF` owns `pOptSurfaceToKeep` and the two faces have opposite surface normals (`bFlipFace == TRUE`), `SmBrep::DeleteEdge` swaps their surfaces and calls `pSurvF->FlipFaceOrientation()` (`:6196`), inverting `pSurvF`'s primary faceuse orientation so it now matches `pDelF`'s. `bSameOrientationFaces` is never updated after that flip, so when `pDelF`'s remaining loops are moved onto `pSurvF` (`:6526-6530`), `if (!bSameOrientationFaces) pMoveLoop->m_pLU = pMoveLoop->m_pLU->GetOtherLoopuse();` flips each moved loop onto the wrong side of `pSurvF`.
- **Trigger:** Merging two coplanar faces with opposite surface normals across a shared closed loop that is inner on one face and outer on the other, passing `pDelF`'s surface as `pOptSurfaceToKeep`, when `pDelF` also carries an inner hole loop. Reached in `tangency-labs/solidcore#2927` (`flanged_spool.step` cut at `z = 130`, where the tool box's `+Z` cap face merges across `r = 35` with the spool's `-Z` step face `r in [31, 35]`, inverting the `r = 31` bore loop on the merged cap face, failing `SmBrep` Pointer Test 98 `Edgeuses share same region` and inflating the integrated volume from `217496.97` to `609976.33`).
- **How found:** `tangency-labs/solidcore#2927`, traced in `topology::brep::p4::delete_edge` and verified directly in `source/SMLib/src/SmBrep.cpp:5872-6531`.
- **Confirmed:** yes, by reading the pinned C++ source (`SmBrep.cpp:5872, 6196, 6526-6530`) and reproducing on the direct port (`wr753_delete_edge_flipping_surviving_face_preserves_moved_inner_loop_orientation` in `crates/solidcore/src/topology/brep/p4.rs` and `cut z > 130` in `crates/step-brep/tests/issue_2927_spool_boolean.rs`). Toggling `same_orientation_faces` when `flip_face_orientation` succeeds eliminates Pointer Test 98 and restores the exact annular area (`96 * pi`) and cut volume (`217496.97`, within `0.29` of the closed form `217496.68`).
