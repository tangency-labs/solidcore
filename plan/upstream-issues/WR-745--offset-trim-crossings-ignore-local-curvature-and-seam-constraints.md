#### WR-745. Offset trimming misclassifies curved contacts and replaces satisfied tangent-seam constraints

- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Offsets and curve/surface intersection classification
- **Location:** `offset::offset_geometry_creation::OffsetGeometryCreation::trim_extended_edge`, WR-666's near-tangent crossing test and trim candidate selection; `surface::surface::p4` contact classifier.
- **What happens:** WR-666 samples a curve on either side of a contact at one thousandth of its entire parameter interval and compares both samples with the contact's tangent plane. On a long curved intersection, surface curvature can put both samples on the same side of that plane even though the curve crosses the actual surface. Conversely, an intersection with a near-coincident extended support can replace an already established tangent-seam point, leaving a short backtracking edge.
- **Trigger:** native synthetic M20x2.5 offset cases from SolidCraft #11582. A continuous flank/cap intersection has parameter length 1666.887; valid cylinder cuts at 85.4335 and 93.9504 degrees are rejected, and projecting the old vertex gives endpoints about .2 from the required radii. In the segmented case, a redundant adjacent support cut at 90.0041 degrees replaces a valid seam point by a point .000751 away, leaving partially coincident edges.
- **How found:** independent native intersection and closest-point probes, and snapshots before gap closing. The underlying bodies are constructed in SolidCraft; no commercial CAD geometry is involved.
- **Confirmed:** in Rust. No C++ reference execution is claimed.
- **Correction:** offset trimming uses the existing local curvature classifier with explicit operation tolerances. A new entry point preserves shallow transverse contacts between local linear sections; legacy callers retain their previous behavior. A previously mated non-lamina tangent-seam point remains a constraint only when it satisfies every adjacent extended support within the supplied tolerance. Failed known seam candidates are removed from the candidate pool; independent surface cuts remain available. There is no retry, tolerance increase or validation bypass.
- **Native regression:** existing geometry-creation tests verify retained candidate selection and removal when an independent support moves. Existing surface p4 tests cover a .6-degree planar crossing, explicit tolerances on unowned curved geometry, and invalid tolerances. The existing WR-666 trough and pocket closed-form tests preserve their exact volumes, manifold status and face counts. The original segmented all-lateral offset is a manifold solid passing strict validation after this correction and the preceding fixes.
- **Geometric context:** the surface normal section differs from its tangent plane. The reused classifier compares curve and surface osculating geometry, including Meusnier curvature; see Patrikalakis, Maekawa and Cho, *Shape Interrogation for Computer Aided Design and Manufacturing*, sections [3.3](https://web.mit.edu/hyperbook/Patrikalakis-Maekawa-Cho/node29.html) and [5.7](https://web.mit.edu/hyperbook/Patrikalakis-Maekawa-Cho/node89.html).
