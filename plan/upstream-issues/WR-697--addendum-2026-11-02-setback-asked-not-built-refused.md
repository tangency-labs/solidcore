Updates: WR-697

#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). A setback asked for at a corner the Setback does not build is dropped without a word
- **Severity:** Wrong result (silent)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (the fork's, after `SmBlendCorner::ClassifyBall` and `ClassifySetBack`) and `SmFilletConvexNxNCorner::MakeCornerTopology` (its setback branches deleted at dc757a6).
- **What happens:** a caller asks for a corner to be set back with `SmFilletExecutive::SetSetBackFill` before the corners are made, or with `AddSetBackVertex` and then `SmFilletCorner::SetSetBackDist` on the made corner. Where the corner is not the blend corner's Setback (curved faces or edges, blends of mixed kinds, or a regular apex of four or more equal rounds, which is the Ball unless the vertex is also an `AddSetBackVertex`), it goes to the Ball or to the N x N corner. Since dc757a6 the N x N corner has no setback path, so `m_dSetBackDist` is ignored and the corner is built as if no setback were asked for, with no error. The fork refuses this only in its capi (`round_edges`, `SMLIB_ERR_UNSUPPORTED`, dc757a6); its SMLib builds the corner without the setback.
- **Source:** SolidCraft review of solidcore #2365 (the port of tangency-labs/smlib#125). This is solidcore-native, with no fork commit: solidcore does not port the fork's capi, so the refusal is made in the kernel.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, one predicate, `blend_corner::setback::asked_setback_not_built` (the corner is not the Setback), and one comment (`SETBACK_NOT_BUILT`), at both doors a caller asks for a setback through:
  - `SetSetBackFill` before the corners are made: in `FilletSession::create_corners`, after the Ball and Setback classifications, a vertex with a `set_set_back_fill` request whose corner is not the Setback is `CornerCreation::Refused` with `FilletErrorType::CornerUnsupported`.
  - `AddSetBackVertex` and `SetSetBackDist` on the made corner (`FilletSession::set_corner_setback`): `do_filleting` refuses first, with the same error and comment, when any corner with a setback (`> 0`) is not the Setback. The setback is set after the corners are made, so this door is checked when the filleting starts.
  Both are decided from the request and the classification only. Without the flag nothing changes.
- **Triggers:** the box [0, 40]^2 x [0, 20] less the cylinder r 20 about (40, 40); at (20, 40, 20) a top edge, the top arc and the vertical edge, all convex, rounded 2 and set back 4. The fork's SMLib at 18e96f1 builds 11 faces, volume 25658.4914490411, without the setback; solidcore now refuses it.
- **Confirmed:** in solidcore. Without the flag the pinned setback corner is built (16 faces), as before.
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/fork_smlib125_open_setbacks.rs`: `setback_asked_where_the_setback_is_not_built_is_refused` (the fill's door) and `setback_set_on_a_corner_that_is_not_the_setback_is_refused` (the corner's door), each flag on (refused) and flag off (the pinned corner). Each fails without its door's check (the corner was built without its setback).
