#### WR-703. A round takes a boss or a cut standing inside its band

- **Severity:** Wrong result (a feature inside the strip of face a round takes is gone from the result, with no error)
- **Status:** Rust opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_keep_features`, off by default. Fixed in the fork at tangency-labs/smlib@eb883ad (smlib#79, #2273); pinned C++ v1.0.1 keeps the defect.
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletExecutive.cpp`, `SmFilletExecutive::DoFilleting`: the fillet sheet is merged into the target, and the merge drops whatever stands on the faces the round takes.
- **Failure:** A 40 x 30 x 20 block with a cylinder of radius 2 standing 2 to 6 in from its front edge, as a boss 5 tall or a hole 5 deep, and that top front edge rounded 8. The result is the block less its round, 23450.619298 mm³ and 7 faces, for both the boss and the hole.
- **Rust port:** `blend::fillet_executive::session::keep_features`, the fork's `SmFilletKeepFeatures.cpp`. A feature is an inner loop of a face a round rolls on whose rim is all concave (a boss) or all convex (a cut), not the blended edge's own loop. Its walls are carried to the round: a cut's own walls, a boss's footprint swept down into the body (walls upright to a planar face). Each sheet face is sectioned by each wall, exactly by a plane, else by a surface intersection within a tenth of the fillet's tolerance. The sheet is split along the creases and its pieces bounded by one feature's creases alone go. The sheet's boundary is imprinted on the body, the faces round the blended edges are deleted up to it and to a kept boss's footprint, and the sheet is sewn in. Nothing is subtracted. With no band reaching a feature the fillet is merged as before.
- **Refused:** A boss whose walls are not upright to a planar face it stands on (`FilletErrorType::NotExact`) when its band reaches it, and a sheet that does not close on the body or sew into it (`FilletErrorType::BadMerge`). A refusal leaves the target as it was, where the fork leaves whatever it reached.
- **Tests:** `tests/fork_eb883ad_keep_features.rs`, the fork's ddbb782 cases: kept, 23528.165493 mm³ with the boss and 23402.501787 mm³ with the hole, 9 faces, the wall on the round through 2 pi; flag off, the block less its round, 7 faces; a round of 1.9 stops short of the feature, 9 faces either way. Kept volumes to 1e-7, as the fork's own at 5e8c9c1: the crease is fitted, which moves the column's volume by about ten times its tolerance.
- **Against the fork:** With every flag on, against the fork's kernel at eb883ad: the same statuses and face counts in all cases; a prism's planar walls and a pocket astride the contact line give the fork's volume in 17 digits, a cone's walls within 3e-12. The cylinders are within 7e-12, and the same bodies before any round already differ there (a cylinder of radius 2 and height 5: 62.831853069045 in the fork, 62.831853069490 here), so that part isn't this code's. Not traced further.
