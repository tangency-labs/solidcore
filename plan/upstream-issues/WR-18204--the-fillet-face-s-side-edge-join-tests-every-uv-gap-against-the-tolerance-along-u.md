#### WR-18204. The fillet face's side-edge join tests every UV gap against the tolerance along u
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a blend that fails)
- **Area:** Fillets
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `JoinTwoFilletVertsByCurves` (`:8604`-`:8812`: the gap tests at `:8762` and `:8765`), with the tolerance `SmFilletGeom::MakeFaceBrep` gives it (`:9300`-`:9305`: `SmTol::MapTo2d` of the approximation tolerance at the middle of the fillet surface, in the UV direction (1, 0)).
- **What happens:** the join chains the fillet geometry's side edges from the end of one rail to the end of the other. In UV it skips a side edge whose start is more than 20 times `dUVTol` from the chain's end, before the 3D distance is looked at. `dUVTol` is the 3D tolerance mapped along u, the direction of the spine, for every gap. A gap across the round runs along v, where a parameter step covers the arc and not the spine's length: there the same 3D distance is a far larger step in UV, so a gap inside the 3D tolerance is skipped as too large. The join then reports no connection, the face is capped round the fillet surface's whole extension, the fillet B-rep has no edge where the side edge lies, and `SetFilletBrepEdgeMap` fails with "Unable to map new Fillet Edge back to Edge in Target Brep" (status 1001).
- **Trigger:** a box 33 x 8.3 x 9.7 with corner (0, -4.15, 0), an equal chamfer of 0.8 on the top edge through (33, 0, 9.7), then a round of radius 0.8 + g, g from 1.5e-5 to 2.5e-5, on the top edge through (16.5, 4.15, 9.7). The round's end edge on the chamfer closes onto the chamfer's corner (33, 4.15, 8.9), which the rail's end passes by g sqrt 2 (2.8e-5 at g = 2e-5, inside the tolerance 4e-5). The fillet surface runs 66 mm per unit of u and 1.26 mm per unit of v, so `dUVTol` is 6.06e-7 and the gap, 1.6e-5 in UV along v, is over 20 times it. At g = 1.3e-5 the gap is under that limit and the round builds; from g = 3e-5 the geometry has no side edge on the chamfer.
- **How found:** a randomly generated assembly; then the join's vertices, side edges and tolerances printed for the three radii.
- **Confirmed:** yes, on the Rust port (test `a_round_just_over_a_neighbouring_chamfer_builds` in `crates/solidcore-ops/tests/blend.rs` fails without the fix). Not run on the C++.
- **Fix:** `uv_tol_towards` (`crates/solidcore/src/blend/fillet_geom/p6.rs`): the join skips a gap as too large in UV beyond 20 times the tolerance in the gap's own direction, `dUVTol` times the ratio of what the 3D tolerance maps to in that direction and along u, at the same point. Along u that is `20 * dUVTol`. A gap over `dUVTol` is kept only when it also passes the 3D test at `:8780`, as upstream (unit test `join_in_uv_judges_a_gap_by_its_direction_and_its_3d_length`). Always on.
- **Not fixed here:** the tolerance is mapped at the middle of the surface, as upstream maps it, not at the gap.
