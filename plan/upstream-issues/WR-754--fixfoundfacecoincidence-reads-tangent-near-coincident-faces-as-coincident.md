#### WR-754. The merge reads two faces as coincident when they only share a tangent edge, so a fillet against a fillet a hair apart is classified on the wrong side
- **Severity:** Wrong result (open or invalid body, or status 1001, on a boolean of two nearly coincident filleted bodies)
- **Area:** Boolean (merge, face coincidence)
- **Location:** at the pin `3fd5b95`: `source/SMLib/src/SmMerge.cpp:2129` (`FixFoundFaceCoincidence`) and the radial-sector search it relies on. A mated edge's radial sector compares the two faces only at the edge, and the faces of the other body found coincident there are taken as coincident faces.
- **What happens:** two fillets (or cones, spheres, tori) tangent at a mated edge but parting further along the surface, by up to the shift between the bodies, read as coincident. The face found "coincident" at the edge gets the other side's region, so the result is not closed or the boolean fails.
- **Trigger:** two 40 x 30 x 10 boxes with R2 vertical fillets, the second shifted by dx along x, both A - B and B - A. dx = 1e-4 and 1e-3 fail in both orders; dx = 1e-2 fails in both orders; dx = 0.1 works.
- **How found:** issue #3012 (a near-coincident cut of filleted bodies).
- **Confirmed:** yes, by the same logic in the C++ (`SmMerge.cpp:2129`); the Rust port had it too.
- **Rust port:** `p1::faces_coincide` gates the coincident-surface merge in `classify_edge_faces` and the coincident face uses in `find_brep_region_of_other_face`: a face counts as coincident only when its surface does (`Surface::coincidence_check`), except for zero-angle sectors (`RadialSector::zero_sector`). Two planes are exempt (the step-off of the sector measures their gap exactly), and so are offset and B-spline surfaces (their coincidence check is not exact enough to overrule the sector). dx = 1e-4, 1e-3: valid, volume dx * 30 * 10 less the tapered ends, both orders; dx = 1e-2: B - A valid, A - B has the right volume but fails `check` with a Vertex/Face gap across an Edgeuse (offset equal to the tolerance at 8 digits), a separate cause not fixed here. Test: `crates/solidcore-ops/tests/issue_3012_filleted_boxes_apart.rs`.
