Updates: WR-160

#### WR-160 (update, 2026-10-31). The four third-order terms are corrected by default
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmOffsetSurface.cpp:1468`, `:1479`, `:1482` and `:1491`, `SmOffsetSurface::EvaluatePlainOrSimple` (the pinned v1.0.1 source; the same four terms in `crates/solidcore/src/surface/offset_surface.rs`).
- **What changed:** solidcore corrects exactly four third-order terms unconditionally:
  1. `Cuuv`'s `Buuv x Buv` coefficient is `1.0`, not upstream's `3.0` (the derivative of `Cuu` gives `2 - 1 = 1`; the two cross-product terms cancel one sign).
  2. `Cuvv`'s `Buvv x Buv` coefficient is `-1.0`, not upstream's `3.0` (the derivative of `Cvv` gives `1 - 2 = -1`).
  3. `Duvv` adds `Buvv` (`d(1, 2)`), not `Buuv` (`d(2, 1)`).
  4. `Dvvv` adds `Bvvv` (`d(0, 3)`), not `Buuv` (`d(2, 1)`).
  The correction can change `Duuv`, `Duvv` and `Dvvv`; downstream effects are unmeasured. Sampled bits for orders 0..2 and `Duuu` are retained in a regression, not a universal bit-identity proof.
- **Evidence:** a new focused unit test uses the biquadratic Bezier base `B(u, v) = (u + u^2 v, v + u v^2, u v)` on `[0, 1]^2`, whose `Bu x Bv = (1 + uv) q` with `q = (-v, -u, 1 + 3uv)` gives both `Buuv x Buv` and `Buvv x Buv` non-zero (a surface of revolution alone cannot reach the `Cuvv` coefficient). The test builds it through the public `BSplineSurface::from_data` (quadratic 3x3 clamped net), constructs a public `OffsetSurface::new` and calls the public `OffsetSurface::evaluate(uv, 3, 3, flags)` with `flags.only_upper_half = true` at `(0.25, 0.5)` and `(0.5, 0.25)` with `d = +0.125` and `-0.125`. This requests total order 3 in the upper triangle of a 4x4 matrix; a full rectangular `(3, 3)` request exceeds the existing derivative bound and is not claimed. The oracle is the independently derived closed-form `B + d q / sqrt(L)`, `L = 1 + u^2 + v^2 + 6uv + 9u^2v^2`, checked to `1e-10` relative without an absolute floor. Before the four corrections the test failed on `Suuv.x` (`1.850528767371623` vs the closed-form `1.740100020590324` at `d = 0.125`, `uv = (0.25, 0.5)`); after them it passes, and a second test retains pre-fix default bits for orders 0..2 and `Suuu` at those four samples.
- **Still unfixed:** `WR-162` (the upper-half, unequal-order second-derivative slot write) is separate and remains. The fix makes no claim about `SM_API` higher derivatives or an end-to-end `CrvOnSurf` improvement; an in-kernel `CrvOnSurf` evaluator that reads these third partials can consume the corrected values, but no production case that does was measured.
- **Confirmed:** the numeric counterexample above was measured on Rust, not newly replayed in C++. The pinned C++ retains these four source terms. The pre-fix log shows two passing geometry/default controls and the failing derivative assertion, but its shell pipeline masked the numeric exit; only the failed test summary, not an actual exit 101, is recorded for that run.
