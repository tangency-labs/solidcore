Updates: WR-685

#### WR-685 (update, 2026-11-02). Where a variable round's radius law has a corner, the round is not the union of its balls there
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the 20 cube's edge y = z = 20 rounded by the law 4 -> 2 -> 4 removes 35.1156 in the pinned build against the union of the balls' 39.5421; the fork before its law corners, with the pieces joined from their own end circles, 39.6456)
- **Area:** Blend (fillet solver)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletStandardSolver.cpp`, `SmCircularCrossSectionFSG::CreateSurface` (the fork's `SmGnSectionFSG::CircleSurface` in `SmFilletSection.cpp`), and the fillet executive's split of a round at its radius corners (fork only: `SmFilletExecutive::NoteRadiusCornerSurfaces` and `SplitFilletFacesAtRadiusCorners`).
- **What happens:** at a corner of the radius law the ball's radius changes slope, and each side of the corner ends on the corner ball's touch circle of its own side (the envelope's `<p - c, c'> = -r r'` with that side's `r'`), both through the same two rail points. The pinned build skins one smooth surface across the corner and overshoots there (#430). The fork, before 7babe17, skinned each side on its own and joined them along one boundary row. Neither is the union of the balls: where the law dips the circle after lies ahead of the one before and the corner ball's own sphere shows between them; where it peaks the sides run past each other and the round ends where they cross.
- **Trigger:** the fork's capi section cases `variable_round_law_dip` (above, also from the other end and as a quadratic law with a corner: 39.542073 removed, nine faces), `variable_round_law_peak` (the 10 x 20 x 30 box's edge x = 10, z = 30, the law 2 -> w -> 2 from y = 0, w 2.5, 3, 3.2 and 4: 21.799882, 27.093683, 29.389966 and 39.541034 removed, eight faces) and `variable_round_law_dips` (the cube's edge, 4, 2, 4, 2 at 0, 0.25, 0.75, 1: a dip then a peak, 38.818277 removed, ten faces), all within 1e-4.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's law corners, tangency-labs/smlib 7babe17, 58d03ef and 62fcc82 (smlib#79, merged into replay/1.0.1 at 5e8c9c1). Tracking: tangency-labs/solidcore#2273, solidcore#430.
- **Confirmed:** against fork 5e8c9c1 built locally (SMLib and SM_API with the harness's flags, `-ffp-contract=off`), through a scratch driver (not committed) that links the fork's capi sources and runs its three cases: the dip 39.542055909305 removed from either end and 39.542055908618 as the quadratic (nine faces), the peaks 21.799877917178, 27.093677260694, 29.389911194516 and 39.541083296321 (eight faces), the dips 38.818141402826 (ten faces), every result sound at check level 2. The pinned build's dip (35.1156, seven faces) and first peak (19.1640) are from the Rust port at default fixes.
- **Rust port (partial):** `solidcore::blend::law_corners`, for `Kernel::fixes.fillet_gn_section_generator` (the fork keeps the pieces only for a ball whose radius varies, inside the G^n section's `CircleSurface`, which the flag already gates):
  - `LawCornerPieces` and `LawCornerKind` (`SmLawCornerPieces`); `LawCornerPieces::classify`, the corner loop of `CircleSurface` (each corner's kind from the middles of the two sides' end sections along the rail, beyond the skin's tolerance; the corner ball's centre and radius; the pieces' accuracy, widened to an approximated arc's share of the radius);
  - `find_law_corner_pieces` and `has_built_law_corners` (58d03ef's `FindLawCornerPieces`, by the surface's C0 knots across u, and `HasBuiltLawCorners`);
  - `sphere_zone` (`sm_SphereZone`: the corner ball's zone between the touch circles as one rational biquadratic patch, poles at the rail points) and `curve_between` (`sm_CurveBetween`).
  - Checked in `crates/solidcore/tests/wr685_law_corners.rs`: the dip's zone matches the fork's `sm_SphereZone` bit for bit (control net and weights) and lies on the corner ball between the two touch circles; the dip, peak and constant laws' pieces, skinned on the touch circles, classify as dip, peak and matching with the closed-form ball (10, 18, 18), r 2, and (10, 16, 16), r 4.
- **Not ported (blocked):** the pieces are not kept and the corners are not built, because the fork builds them on its radius corners, tangency-labs/smlib@6eecf04 and 5fbffc9, which solidcore does not have: the trace's through points and one-sided tangents at the law's corners (solidcore#1403, parked), the piecewise skin and join in `CircleSurface` (noted as not ported in the WR-685 addendum of 2026-11-01), and the split of the round's faces at its corners (`NoteRadiusCornerSurfaces`, `SplitFilletFacesAtRadiusCorners`, with `SmBrep::SubdivideFaceAtDiscontinuities` and `ReplaceCurve`, solidcore#1828). Waiting on them: `SmFilletExecutive::BuildLawCorners` (with `sm_PieceOfFace`, `sm_EdgeBetween`, `SmBrep::UnStitch` and 62fcc82's choice of the new faces), the pieces' copy in `NoteRadiusCornerSurfaces`, and 58d03ef's refusal of a law-cornered round in `EaseSetBackSections`.
