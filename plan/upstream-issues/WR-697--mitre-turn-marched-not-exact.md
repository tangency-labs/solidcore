#### WR-697. The mitre of two equal cylinder rounds at a sharp turn is a marched intersection, 1.4e-5 off both rounds, and `MakeFaceBrep` drops the fillet faces' own trim curves
- **Severity:** Wrong result (accuracy)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletNx2Corner::MakeCornerTopology` (the bevel branch at a corner of three edges) and `SmFilletEdge::CalcFilletIntFillet` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`), which marches the mitre with `sm_SrfSrfIntersection`; `SmFilletGeom::MakeFaceBrep` (`source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`), whose `SmBrep::MakeFaceWithCurves` call passes no `pbOptCrvOnSurf`.
- **What happens:** where two rounds of a chain turn at a sharp side edge (a boss's base at its vertical edge), each runs on to the other and they are cut along their crossing, the mitre. Where both are constant circular rounds of one radius on straight spines whose axes meet, between planes, the mitre is the plane of symmetry's section of either cylinder: an ellipse arc. Upstream marches the two rounds' intersection and fits it: 1.386e-5 off the cylinders on the trigger below, its trim curves 1.40e-5 off it, and the body's volume 1.0e-9 relative off its closed form. `MakeFaceBrep` hands `MakeFaceWithCurves` each edge's trim curve on the fillet surface, and `MakeFaceWithCurves` keeps them only under `pbOptCrvOnSurf`: every fillet face's trim curves are made again later by projecting the edges' curves.
- **Trigger:** a 40 x 30 x 10 plate (origin at 0) united with a 10 x 10 x 5 boss at (15, 10, 10); the boss's four base edges rounded r 1 together (constant radius, circular section). Closed-form volume `12500 + 40 (1 - pi/4) + 8 (5/6 - pi/4)` = 12508.967554823590.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's smlib#79 step 4b (tangency-labs/smlib 74e9beb, 48a8a9f, 32fd80a): `SmBlendCorner` takes over the Nx2 corner's corners of two blends of one convexity (a Join or a Turn; the two-blend construction moved unchanged), and its `ExactMitreCurve` builds the exact mitre; `MakeFaceBrep` passes `pbOptCrvOnSurf` TRUE. The fork's capi case `turn mitre_boss_base`.
- **Confirmed:** in the port (`crates/solidcore/tests/wr697_blend_corner_turn.rs`). The fork 5e8c9c1, built locally and run on the trigger through `SmFilletExecutive` (as capi's `round_edges` calls it), prints volume 12508.967554846866, 15 faces, and each mitre 1.26e-15 to 3.77e-15 off the bisector plane and 2.44e-15 to 3.55e-15 off the cylinders; the port with the flag prints the same values to the printed digits. The pinned C++ was not run on the trigger: the port's flag-off path is the pinned Nx2 corner, at difftest parity.
- **Rust port:** opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, off by default. With it on:
  - a vertex with two blended edge uses of one convexity among three or four edges is classified (`SmBlendCorner::Classify`): a Join where the edges continue each other (G1) or meet at 5 degrees or less at a face corner of the other convexity, else a Turn, Mitred. The corner is built by the Nx2 port, as the fork's moved code builds it (`crate::blend::blend_corner`, module notes);
  - at a Mitre turn of three edges the mitre (`SM_FE_FILLET_X_FILLET`, no curve yet) is made exact before the corner's edges are computed (`exact_mitre_curve`): the rational quadratic arc in the plane of symmetry, X (the axes' crossing's foot on the common face) and S (the rails on the walls meeting the side edge's line) placed exactly, and exact rational cubic UV curves on both rounds, checked on the rounds at seven parameters to 1e-12 of the size. Anything else keeps the marched curve;
  - `MakeFaceBrep` keeps the fillet faces' own trim curves.
- **What the flag changes:** on the trigger, the volume 12508.967542131706 becomes 12508.967554846866 (1.9e-12 relative off the closed form), the mitres 1.386e-5 off the cylinders become 3.6e-15, their trim curves 1.40e-5 off become 3.4e-14. The port's own projection of the exact mitre onto the cylinder rounds is within 3.5e-14 on the trigger, so this case does not distinguish the `MakeFaceBrep` part (the fork measured its projection 6.1e-6 off).
- **Tests:** `crates/solidcore/tests/wr697_blend_corner_turn.rs`.
