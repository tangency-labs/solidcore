#### WR-697 (update, 2026-11-02). A round ending where its edge runs on smoothly, a mixed End whose rail passes over its side edge, a trim across an edge of more than two faces, and a rail end past a seam's vertex
- **Severity:** Wrong result; failure (status 1001)
- **Area:** Fillets (corners)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletCorner.cpp`, `SmFilletCorner::Create` (a vertex where the blended edge runs on G1 into an unblended edge of the same two faces builds `SmFilletNx1Corner`, whose end rib needs a side edge that is not there); `source/SMLib/src/SmFilletGeom.cpp`, `SmFilletGeom::TrimOriginalSurfaces` (collects the faces the rails enclose across every edge, including one of more than two faces), `SmFilletVertex::CalcRailIntEdgeuse` (a rail end is looked for on the side edge only) and `SmFilletEdge::CalcEdgeOnEdge`.
- **What happens:**
  - Where the blended edge ends at a vertex V and runs on there G1 between the same two faces (a bore's rim arc running into the rest of the rim at the cylinder's seam), the pinned corner fails (1001).
  - Where a trim's rails enclose faces next to an edge of more than two faces (the bore's wall touching the bottom face along a line, tangency-labs/solidcore#453), the faces are collected across it into the whole body. The trim then leaks, and the blend's faces are merged into a body left whole.
  - A concave round's End of mixed convexity whose rail on the side face passes over the side edge of the other convexity (the junction rig's boss tilted out of the plate, its cap rising into air) builds material over that face.
  - A side edge split only by a seam that ends on it (a cylinder's seam on its cap's rim) stops the rail's end search at the seam's vertex, and the corner fails.
- **How found:** the SolidCraft fork's smlib#124, landed in replay/1.0.1 at 619c2f0: d657afa (`TrimOriginalSurfaces`), 41a39ec (the End at a smooth vertex), 54a4ee7 (the mixed End's overflow refusal, `BallTouchingAt`), 63c7962 (the side edge continued across a seam's vertex). The fork's capi tests (e4a4ac0, a889295, 17ea413, 4b8a1e7, ce52279, ad30d68) gave the inputs and expectations only.
- **Rust port:** under the same opt-in `Kernel::fixes.fillet_blend_corner`, with no new flag.
  - **d657afa:** `select_and_delete_with_marks` (`session/original_trim.rs`) marks an edge of more than two faces as a trim bound. A trim that still leaks is `SM_FILERR_BAD_MERGE`: "the faces a fillet's rails enclose on the body reach past its rails (the trim leaks)".
  - **41a39ec:** `blend::blend_corner::end::end_at_smooth_vertex` classifies the End (`BlendEnd::AtSmooth`, kind `NByTwo`). `FilletCorner::make_end_at_smooth_vertex` builds its topology: the blend is cut by the plane through V square to the edge (`FilletCorner::end_cap`), and the rails end on that plane (`calc_rail_int_end_surface` and the side-face intersection use the cap). The end rib is the blend's section there, and the cap's other edges are the side faces' traces (`calc_end_cap_edges`). A blend End's edges are `calc_end_edge_geom`, the fork's `CalcEndEdgeGeom`, instead of `drop_end_rib`. A corner that noted its reason (or `SM_FILERR_NO_ROOM`) keeps it in `calculate_corner_edges`.

  - **63c7962:** `side_edgeuse_across_seam_vertex` and `side_edge_chain_to` (at most `SIDE_EDGE_CHAIN_STEPS` = 64 steps; the fork's loop has the same bound). `calc_rail_int_edgeuse_with_solvers` continues a missed rail end on the next edge across a seam's vertex. Its recursion depth is bounded at the same 64, where the fork has no bound. `calc_edge_on_edge_at_corner` joins the chain from V to the rail's end (`BSplineCurve::create_by_joining`).
