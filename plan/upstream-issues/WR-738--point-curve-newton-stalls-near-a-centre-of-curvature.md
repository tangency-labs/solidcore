#### WR-738. The point/curve closest-point Newton step stalls near a centre of curvature, so no closest point on an arc is found from near its centre
- **Severity:** Wrong result
- **Area:** Curves
- **Location:** `SmFindPCExtremaEFO::Evaluate`, the function `SmCurve::LocalPointSolve` solves for `SM_SO_MINIMIZE` (and so every `GlobalPointSolve` of a B-spline curve through its leaf spans): the second order term of `F' = C'.C' + (C - P).C''` is limited to `-0.95 C'.C'` wherever it is lower ([B52], "so that it cannot change the sign of the step").
- **What happens:** near a centre of curvature the true `F'` is small and positive: for a point `rho` from the centre of an arc of radius `R`, `F' = C'.C' rho / R` at the closest point. The limit raises it to `0.05 C'.C'`, so every step is `(rho / R) / 0.05` of Newton's: the iteration converges linearly at the rate `1 - 20 rho / R` and runs out of its 100 iterations from within about `R / 100` of the centre. `LocalPointSolve` returns no answer, and the curve/point global solver, which gives up on a span when its first guess fails, finds no closest point on the arc at all, though it is unique (the arc's point at the test point's angle). On the R5 quarter arc of a rounded box's bottom loop: found from 0.05 and 0.1 off the centre, nothing from 0.02, 0.01, 1e-3, 1e-4 or 1e-6.
- **Trigger:** tangency-labs/solidcore#2546 (SolidCraft #9522): a 30 x 30 x 30 box with its vertical edges rounded R5, its bottom loop chamfered d = 4.99 with `fillet_chamfer_cone_on_arc`. The corner where an arc's chamfer meets a line's (`SM_FV_RAIL_X_EDGEUSE`) starts its search for where the rail crosses the round's seam with `LoadInitialValues`, which drops the ball's centre, 5 - d = 0.01 from the round's axis, onto the filleted arc; the drop finds nothing, `LoadInitialValues` fails, and the fillet ends with a bare status 1001 from `CreateFilletCorners`.
- **How found:** the #2546 case, traced to `cr_initial_body`'s edge drop, then reproduced on the loop's B-spline alone.
- **Confirmed:** in the port, which follows upstream's limit and iteration count; not run against the C++.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.curve_point_minimize_full_newton`, `FindPcExtremaEfo::evaluate` (`crates/solidcore/src/curve/curve/p3.rs`) limits the term only when minimising where the full `F'` is not positive (the point at or past the centre of curvature, where the stationary point is no minimum), the case [B52]'s limit is for; elsewhere the step is Newton's. On a circle Newton's step from angle `a` off the answer is `-tan(a)` whatever `rho` is, so it converges from any guess within the span. Maximisation keeps upstream's limit. Solidcore-native, no fork commit. It changes the last digits of closest points that upstream found slowly (within `R / 20` of a centre), hence the flag.
