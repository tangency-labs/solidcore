#### UB-271. `SmTopologySweep::SweepEdgeHigh` reads the owner of an original edge curve the face creator deleted (sub-tolerance closed profile at the rotation axis)
- **Severity:** Crash / undefined behaviour
- **Area:** Topology
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTopologySweep.cpp:2165`, `SmTopologySweep::SweepEdgeHigh` (def. `:1943`): `pOrigEdgeCurve->GetOwner()` after `SmBrep::MakeFaceWithCurves` (`:2129`). Reached from `SmPrimitiveCreation::CreateRotationalSweep` (`source/SMLib/src/SmPrimitiveCreation.cpp:2852`; both the capped `DoSweep` of the faces and the uncapped `DoSweep` of the edges, `:3006`-`:3022`) through `SmApiCreateRotationalSweep` and `SmApiCreateRotationalSweepWithRepetitions` (`source/SM_API/src/SmApiBrep.cpp:1254`, `:1303`).
- **What happens:** use-after-free. `SmRotationalSweepGeometry::EdgeSweepHigher` hands back the copy of the original edge curve as the first trimming curve and `SweepEdgeHigh` keeps the pointer in `pOrigEdgeCurve`; `MakeFaceWithCurves` consumes the trimming curves and, for the sub-tolerance loop below, does not keep that one alive, so the "center line curve goes away" read of its owner dereferences freed memory. SIGSEGV (`KERN_INVALID_ADDRESS`, "possible pointer authentication failure" on arm64) at the same instruction for every variant below.
- **Trigger:** a partial rotational sweep (`dRotAngleDeg < 359.99999`, so not the full-turn `CreateRotationalSweep(curves, base, axis)`) of a profile curve whose extent is below the B-rep tolerance (`SM_ZONE_TOL_3D`, 1e-5) and whose start vertex lies within about 5e-6 of the axis or sweeps an arc shorter than the tolerance (distance from the axis times the angle below 1e-5). Measured with `SmApiCreateRotationalSweepWithRepetitions` (3 repetitions, 90°, axis `(1,0,0)` through the origin, `bCapEnds = FALSE`): an `SmApiCreateCircle` B-spline circle of radius 1.566e-6 (the fuzz case; radius 1.7e-6 crashes, 1.75e-6 returns `SM_ERR`) centred 3.03e-6 from the axis crashes at 30°, 90°, 180°, 270°, 300°, 350°, 359°, 359.99°, -90° and 0°, as does the same circle on the axis; centred 6e-6 away it crashes at 45° and 90° (vertex arcs 4.7e-6 and 9.4e-6) and returns `SM_ERR` at 120° and 180°; 6.5e-6 away: 45° crashes, 90° (arc 1.02e-5) and 300° return `SM_ERR`; 4e-6 away crashes at 359.99°, 5e-6 and 5.5e-6 away return `SM_ERR` there (5e-6 still crashes at 120°); 2e-5 away at 10°, 1e-4 at 1°, 1e-3 at 0.1° and 1 at 1e-4° (arcs 1.7e-6 to 3.5e-6) crash; 1 away at 1e-6° returns `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT` (the degenerate-surface check comes first). The analytic `SmCircle` of that radius crashes on the axis (3.03e-6 away it returns `SM_ERR`); an `SmApiCreateArc` of 0° to 359° crashes (0° to 350° and shorter arcs survive with a one-face result); an `SmApiCreateCurve` loop of five control points 1e-6 to 2e-6 from its centre crashes (3e-6 survives), open or with its last control point up to 4e-6 from the first (8e-6 returns `SM_ERR_AXIS_INSIDE_FACE`). `bCapEnds = TRUE` with one repetition crashes the same; 0 repetitions sweep nothing; a second curve in the list does not help unless it moves the planar-face creation (two tiny circles 1e-5 apart return `SM_ERR_AXIS_INSIDE_FACE`). The same tiny circle 1 away from the axis builds a 3-face body; radius 2e-5 near the axis builds one too; a tiny line or half-circle at the axis survives.
- **How found:** nightly `difftest --unit sweep.api_brep --fuzz 300` of 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-26 (`fuzz00043`: generated case `gen0158` with `/cap = false`), then adversarial probes of the regime.
- **Confirmed:** yes. Linux x86-64 CI: `cpp: killed by signal 11`. macOS arm64 (Apple clang build of the v1.0.1 pin, `-O2 -g`): crash reports of a stand-alone program calling the API and of the difftest shim both fault at `SmTopologySweep.cpp:2165` for the circle, the analytic circle, the 359° arc and the control-point loop.
- **Rust port:** not affected (safe Rust): `sweep::topology_sweep` works with typed ids, and the port returns `SM_ERR_NULL_POINTER` (1006) for every input above, without a panic. The difftest FFI refuses them before the call (`crates/solidcore-ffi/src/units/sweep_api_brep.rs`, `check_rotational_curves`: a profile curve of extent below 1e-5 whose end point is within 2e-5 of the axis or sweeps an arc below 2e-5, where the sweep runs), and the runner refuses them on the Rust side the same way; corpus `crates/difftest/tests/fuzz_corpus/sweep.api_brep.json`.
