Adds SurfaceKind::OffsetSurface, kernel-aware copy/split/extension/planarity dispatch, state-preserving STEP inversion and database dispatch; Surface::create_offset_surface dispatches to the plane, revolution (with approx_tol_3d) and offset overrides; an owning offset is its base's owner (BASE_OWNER, upstream m_pSurface->SetOwner(this)) and hands down the face its own owner chain reaches, so the base's GetFace-based tolerances follow upstream (a face-owned offset's expanded box returns Unported(topology.face_p2) instead of the default zone tolerance; LocalPointSolve_1 on the base takes GetZoneTol3d(GetOwner()) as its loose tolerance), and a non-owning offset's base keeps its caller's owner (Surface::clone_keeping_owner); SmSurface::IsPeriodic ignores Evaluate statuses as upstream (Surface::evaluate_ignoring_status; curve-bounded and STEP surfaces pass the array on to their basis); step_inversion_extending stores the extension an offset base's inversion makes.
